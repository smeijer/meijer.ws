**GoWion** was a specialized workflow and geospatial management platform that I founded and single-handedly developed. Designed to help pipeline managers comply with Dutch WION law, it streamlined processes around **Klic Meldingen** and became a trusted tool for clients like Vermilion Oil and Gas, Zeeland Refinery, Rijksuniversiteit Groningen, Sara Lee, and others.

GoWion's success and innovative approach later led to its evolution into **Clearly.app**, a platform widely used by Dutch municipalities for large-scale infrastructure projects.

---

## Challenge
Pipeline managers in the Netherlands faced unique challenges under WION compliance:
- Efficiently managing **Klic Meldingen** (excavation notifications) and ensuring legal compliance.
- Coordinating tasks while maintaining traceability for audits.
- Visualizing excavation areas and affected zones to enable better planning and communication.

GoWion needed to address these pain points with a user-friendly platform tailored to WION requirements.

---

## Solution
As both the founder and sole engineer, I built GoWion to be a comprehensive solution for WION compliance:
- **Workflow Automation**: Streamlined processes around the lifecycle of a Klic Melding, ensuring every step was tracked and legally compliant.
- **Geospatial Visualization**: Integrated map components provided a clear overview of excavation areas and affected zones, simplifying planning and task coordination.
- **Task Tracking and Traceability**: Each notification, action, and response was logged, making compliance audits seamless.
- **Field Operability**: Mobile-friendly features allowed teams to access and update workflows while on-site, breaking the reliance on office-based systems.

The platform was built on a **C# .NET backend**, providing the scalability and reliability required for enterprise clients.

---

## Results
GoWion quickly gained adoption among major organizations, including:
- Vermilion Oil and Gas, Zeeland Refinery, and Rijksuniversiteit Groningen.
- Delivered full compliance with WION law by simplifying the management of Klic Meldingen.
- Offered geospatial insights that improved planning and coordination for excavation projects.
- Enhanced traceability and accountability, reducing errors and ensuring smooth audits.

## Lessons Learned
1. **Founding and Engineering a Product**: Developing GoWion from idea to product validated my ability to identify industry needs and deliver effective solutions independently.
2. **Customer-Centric Development**: Direct engagement with high-profile clients highlighted the importance of tailoring features to meet user-specific requirements.
3. **The Power of Specialized Tools**: Building a product focused on a specific legal and operational niche ensured immediate and lasting value for users.

---

## Conclusion
GoWion was a pioneering tool in WION compliance, providing pipeline managers with workflows and geospatial insights tailored to their unique needs. Its success eventually paved the way for its rebranding and evolution into **Clearly.app**, demonstrating the long-term value of a product built with focus and precision.
